HOW TO USE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Ask these questions at the right moment — listed with slide number
- Wait 5 seconds after asking before continuing
- For screen share sessions — say "type yes or no in chat"
- Acknowledge every answer — say "exactly" or "great point"
- If no one answers — answer it yourself and move on
- Keep it to 1-2 responses per question — do not let it drag

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  GOLDEN RULES FOR ASKING QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.  After asking — wait 5 full seconds before continuing.
    Silence feels long to you but normal to the audience.

2.  Acknowledge every answer.
    Say: "Exactly", "Great point", "Yes that is a common one"
    Never say: "Wrong" or "Not quite"

3.  If no one answers after 5 seconds — answer it yourself.
    Say: "In my experience..." and move on.

4.  One or two responses per question is enough.
    Do not let one person dominate.

5.  For screen share — say "type yes or no in chat" instead
    of raise of hands. Much easier to see.

6.  Do not ask too many questions on one slide.
    One or two per slide is the right pace.

7.  If someone gives a long answer — say "great, let me
    come back to that after the session" and keep moving.

8.  The best questions are the ones where you already know
    the answer — and it leads into your next point perfectly.
