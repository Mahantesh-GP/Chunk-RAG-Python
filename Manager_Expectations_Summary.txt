MANAGER EXPECTATIONS — SUMMARY
================================
For: Team / Colleague briefing
Date: April 2026


WHAT MANAGER EXPECTS
---------------------
We are building more dashboards going forward. Right now there is no
proper process for how we handle them. Manager wants us to define a
simple generic process that works for any dashboard or application
we build or maintain.


3 KEY THINGS HE SAID
---------------------

1. Think bigger — not just validation
   We were thinking only about testing and validation.
   He wants the full picture — from the moment a requirement comes in
   to deployment and maintenance.
   Validation is just one step inside that.

2. Make it generic
   Do not tie it to Excel or the audit dashboard.
   Tomorrow it could be a database, a SharePoint page, anything.
   The process should work for all of them.

3. Keep it simple
   He does not want a detailed document with hundreds of checks.
   Just key steps, key handoffs, key checkpoints.
   Like notes on paper. Simple enough that any new team member
   can follow it.


THE PROCESS HE HAS IN MIND
---------------------------

   Requirement comes in
         ↓
   Understand it
   (purpose, data source, frequency, stakeholders)
         ↓
   Check code into repo
   Create work item in Azure DevOps
         ↓
   Build it
   (PR review, no self approval)
         ↓
   Validate critical scenarios
   (manual first, automate if recurring)
         ↓
   Deploy
   (notify stakeholders, confirm team ownership)
         ↓
   Maintain
   (bugs, vulnerabilities, enhancements — same cycle)


WHY HE WANTS THIS
-----------------
Because dashboards are growing. If there is no process, every new
dashboard will be handled differently by different people.
He wants one consistent way the whole team follows — not dependent
on any one person.


WHAT HE DOES NOT WANT
----------------------
- A 6 sheet Excel with hundreds of validation checks
- Deep technical documentation
- Individual names assigned — team ownership only
- Perfection — deliver fast and good enough


ONE LINE SUMMARY
----------------
Simple process. Generic. One page. Key steps only.
